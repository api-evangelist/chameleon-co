The Loom AI tour is a seamless and engaging introduction to the platform's capabilities. With each step, it keeps the messaging clear, highlighting the value of AI doing the heavy lifting—whether you're writing a document, creating an issue in Jira or Linear, or sharing a message. The tone is encouraging, guiding users through simple actions with a hint of excitement for the convenience it brings, turning what would be manual tasks into effortless AI-powered processes.

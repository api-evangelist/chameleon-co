The ChatGPT modal introduces users to the flagship LLM model, GPT-4o, highlighting its capabilities in a clear and concise manner. Instead of flooding users with excessive information, the design keeps it simple—just enough detail to generate interest while maintaining low cognitive load. To introduce the "Explore GPTs" feature, ChatGPT leverages a tour component rather than a modal—a strategic choice that allows users to continue their workflow while subtly directing attention.

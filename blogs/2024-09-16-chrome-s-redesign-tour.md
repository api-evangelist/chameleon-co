Google Chrome’s customization tour is a gentle nudge towards personalization, guiding users step-by-step without being intrusive. It’s simple but lacks depth—ideal for casual users but easily overlooked by those less interested in customization. While it highlights useful options, the impact may be fleeting for users not actively seeking a new look.

Figma’s microsurvey is a quick, thoughtful touch to gather feedback on file navigation. The first question asks users to rate how easy it is to find files, followed by an optional second question offering space for specific suggestions. This flow is effective because it’s brief, respectful of the user’s time, and leaves room for deeper insights while maintaining a smooth, non-intrusive experience.

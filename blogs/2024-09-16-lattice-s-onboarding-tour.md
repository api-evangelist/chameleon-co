Lattice Reviews kicks off with a friendly, “Let’s get you started” vibe, offering a choice between a 5-step tour or a short video. The onboarding flow smartly tailors itself to your learning style, so whether you’re the “let’s dive in” type or just browsing, there’s an option for you. This flow wins points for keeping things easy and letting users feel in control—no pressure, just a smooth intro to performance reviews that doesn’t overload you with info.

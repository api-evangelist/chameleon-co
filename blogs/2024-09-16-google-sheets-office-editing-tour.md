Google Sheets introduces its Office Editing feature with a helpful modal, allowing users to edit and share Office-formatted files directly in Docs, Sheets, and Slides. The tooltip provides a smooth step-by-step guide through key actions like converting files, viewing in Office format, and managing version history. This flow works well because it’s simple, clear, and guides users through powerful new options without overwhelming them, ensuring they can seamlessly handle Office files without needing external apps.

The Typeform interface efficiently integrates a subtle yet effective upsell through its coachmark. The suggestion to "Discover VideoAsk" feels more like a helpful recommendation than a pushy sales tactic. It’s nestled within the existing workflow, allowing users to explore a new feature without interrupting their tasks.
